How it's made

Made by hand, one small batch at a time

Simple ingredients and a few careful steps. Here is how a batch goes from jar to bottle.

  1. 1
    SteepVanilla beans or locally roasted 100% Arabica coffee steep in New Zealand vodka.
  2. 2StrainThe finished extract is poured through a fine mesh strainer.
  3. 3Hand-bottleEach amber bottle is filled by hand through a small funnel.
  4. 4Cap & labelCapped, labeled and ready for the market table.
